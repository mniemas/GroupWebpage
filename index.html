Our Mission Statement

We strive to create a collaborative, inclusive, and innovative environment where team members feel empowered to push boundaries and achieve excellence in their craft. Our mission is to deliver high-quality solutions that meet and exceed our clients' expectations.

Team Panda is dedicated to uncovering insights through the analysis of unique and unconventional datasets. We value technical precision and the collaborative pursuit of excellence in computer science to deliver impactful data-driven solutions.

Team Rules

  • Commitment: All team members will complete assignments by the agreed deadlines.
  • Courage: Everyone can share their thoughts and concerns and ask for help whenever needed.
  • Focus: Team members will work on agreed-upon tasks and completely finish their work before moving on.
  • Openness: Team members will share their feedback and be open to new ideas or changes.
  • Respect: Team members will apply criticism in a constructive manner.
  • Meetings: Held Wednesdays 5:00-6:00 PM and Thursdays 2:00-3:00 PM via Zoom.
  • Accountability: If a member is uncooperative, we will reach out directly; if issues persist, a TA will be contacted.
  • Adaptability: If the team falls behind, we will reassess the workload and create a new plan to meet deadlines.
